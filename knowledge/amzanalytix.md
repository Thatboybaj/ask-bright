# AMZAnalytix — Framer website for an Amazon consultancy

AMZAnalytix is an Amazon seller consultancy. Bright built their website in Framer and redesigned the homepage based on client feedback.

## Decisions
- Kept the site to only 2 CMS collections to keep the client's Framer plan cheaper.
- Built the 4 service pages as standalone pages sharing one structure, so they are consistent and easy to update.
