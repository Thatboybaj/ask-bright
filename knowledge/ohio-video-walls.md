# Ohio Video Walls — three homepage concepts

For Ohio Video Walls, Bright designed three Framer homepage concepts, each with full copy across nine sections.

## The three directions
- Industry Standard: confident and familiar.
- Midnight Museum: dark, gallery-like, dramatic.
- Clean Authority: minimal and trustworthy.

Giving the client three tonal directions up front helped them choose a brand voice before the full build.
