# AA Collective — Framer website for a brand consultancy

AA Collective is a brand positioning and merchandising consultancy. Bright designed and built their website in Framer.

## Approach
- Luxury fashion aesthetic, using editorial references such as The Row and Toteme.
- Pure grayscale color palette for a quiet, premium feel.
- Typography: tested Bodoni Moda pairings and script fonts, settling on Snell Roundhand for minimal luxury accents.
- Five core sections on the site.

## Challenge
The original brief and a later client meeting summary conflicted. Bright reconciled the two before building so the client signed off on one clear direction.
