# Pyratech

Climate Tech

'25

Concept

Webflow development

A Webflow website for a company turning hard-to-recycle plastic into clean hydrogen and high-performance carbon.

© Pyratech

## About

Pyratech specializes in transforming plastic waste into circular solutions that power the industries of the future. Founded with the ambition to redefine the life cycle of plastic waste, Pyratech leverages advanced microwave technology to transform industrial-grade plastic into valuable resources: clean hydrogen and high-performance carbon.

## The Objective

I was tasked as Web Designer and Web Developer to create an impactful website for the company. The website's primary goal is to showcase their expertise in turning hard-to-recycle plastic waste into essential components for tomorrow's advanced companies. They envisioned a user-friendly, visually appealing, and responsive website that embodies their commitment to excellence.

## My Solution

My approach was centered around creating a digital presence that resonated with the target audience.

## User-Centric Design

I focused on crafting a user-centric design that felt engaging and approachable. This included vibrant visuals, intuitive navigation, and an interface that appealed to both the younger demographic and those in the technology industry.

## Subtle Interactions

To make the site more interactive, I added subtle interactive features such as 3D animation for the menu and other in-page interactions.

## Responsive Development

Knowing that accessibility across devices was crucial, I ensured that the website was fully responsive and optimised for mobile devices to reach a broader audience.

## The Result

The result was a dynamic and visually captivating website that transforms the way industries perceive plastic waste recycling, bridging the gap between tradition and modernity. This project exemplifies my commitment to creating website solutions that meet and exceed the unique objectives of my clients.
