## Overview

Verdant is a website template for AI studios, venture builders, product agencies and consultancies. It turns a studio’s thesis, ventures and thinking into a site that feels confident and editorial instead of another dark AI landing page.

## My Role

Self-initiated product, owned end to end:

Concept, positioning and art direction for the AI studio niche.

Visual design, type system and design tokens.

Astro build in TypeScript, with Keystatic as the visual CMS.

Motion system, preloader, full-screen menu and showreel player.

Responsive layouts, SEO setup and accessibility.

## Design Approach

I went light instead of dark. A soft mint ground, one sharp acid-green accent and a near-black surface for the heavier sections. Clash Display carries the headlines with tight tracking, and Inter keeps the body copy calm. A network graph runs through the hero and showreel as the visual motif: ideas connecting into products.

## Pages

Home, Services and About.

Ventures with filters, plus a case study page for each venture.

Insights blog with filters and article pages.

Contact, 404, Privacy, Terms, Cookies and a style guide.

## Build

Astro 7 and TypeScript, with no UI framework shipped to the public site.

Keystatic CMS at /keystatic for ventures, insights, team, services, FAQs, jobs and legal pages.

Word-by-word headlines, image wipes, number counters, smooth scrolling and page transitions.

Branded preloader on first visit, smart sticky navigation and a full-screen video showreel.

Optimised WebP images, Open Graph, JSON-LD, sitemap, RSS and reduced-motion support.

Outcome

A 13-page Astro template with a visual CMS for ventures, insights, team, services, FAQs and jobs. Zero UI framework on the public site, so it stays fast, with motion that gives it character.
