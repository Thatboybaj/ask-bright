# Sable Records

Record Label

Framer Marketplace

A dark, editorial Framer template for independent record labels, where one CMS entry powers a release across the homepage, catalogue, artist discography and its own detail page.

© Sable Records

## Overview

Sable Records (SBL.) is a premium Framer template for independent record labels, artist collectives and music imprints, available on the Framer Marketplace . It is dark and editorial by design: oversized serif type, catalogue numbers and full-bleed photography put the records first and keep the interface out of the way.

The site runs entirely on the CMS. A label adds a release once, and it shows up on the homepage, in the catalogue, on the artist's discography and on its own detail page with tracklist, pressing details and credits.

## The Challenge

Record labels publish in a structured way. A release has a catalogue number, a format, an edition, a pressing and a list of credits. Most templates ignore that and treat a record like a blog post with a cover image.

The goal was to model that structure properly without making the site feel like a database. It had to look like a label with taste, and be simple enough for a small team to update without touching the design.

## Design Approach

I took cues from record sleeves and catalogue sheets. Large serif headlines sit on a near-black canvas, while small details like catalogue numbers and pressing info do the quiet work. Photography runs full-bleed so the artwork carries each page.

Motion adds character, not noise. Titles reveal letter by letter on scroll, catalogue text scrambles into place, and release images warp like a spinning record on hover.

## CMS Architecture

Releases: catalogue number, format, edition, pressing, price, buy links and credits.

Tracks: linked to releases and split into Side A and Side B.

Artists: bio, discography, booking details and streaming links.

Gallery: categorised images for shows, sessions and artwork.

Format and Gallery category lists to keep filtering consistent.

Releases, tracks and artists are linked by reference, so a single update flows everywhere it appears. That is what makes the template practical for a real label.
