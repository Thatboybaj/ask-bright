## Overview

GPN Studios is a modern creative hub providing photography, podcast, and music production studios. The goal was to build a clean, premium online presence that reflects the studio’s brand experience and makes booking effortless for creators.

## Challenge

GPN Studios needed a website that:

Showcases studio spaces in a visually compelling way

Communicates professionalism and trust to creators

Makes online booking intuitive and friction-free

Highlights different studio offerings clearly (Photo, Podcast, Music)

Reinforces credibility through real testimonials

Scales easily for future blog and content plans

## Solution

I designed and developed a conversion-focused, minimal, and high-impact website using Framer.

## Key focus areas

Premium & minimal visual direction

High-quality gallery and space presentation

Clear and segmented studio offering pages

Simple booking CTA flow repeated across the site

Social proof to build trust

Future-ready blog and newsletter integration

## Process

## 1. Research & Strategy
