# Countersign

Brand, website and motion for a payment verification startup

Countersign (self initiated)

Brand, Web design, Development, Motion

A concept company that puts a second signature on every payment. It checks the request, the person and the bank account before money moves.

Live Website

© Countersign

Full Casestudy

Typography: Funnel Display for headlines, Inter Tight for text, JetBrains Mono for evidence. Gloock, Instrument Serif and Gilda Display were tested and rejected. The serifs felt like a law firm; this needed to feel like a checkpoint.

Motion: studied Harness, Huly and North. Content rises 60px with a soft ease, staggered like a report being read. The guilloche turns continuously, examiner marks draw in sequence, and stamps land with a small overshoot. Everything respects reduced motion.

The Inspector: a client side rules engine that flags lookalike domains, reply to mismatches, bank detail changes, foreign IBANs, urgency, secrecy and authority pressure, then marks each one in the original email. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Delivery: designed and built solo, from brand board to deployed site, plus a 29 second launch reel written in code with Remotion.

A brand built on proof

A brand that borrows from banknote security printing: guilloche rosettes, a double C mark for the second signature, accounting's double rule and a rubber stamp for every verified payment.

An eleven page Astro site with GSAP motion, a working email Inspector that marks six fraud tells and runs entirely in the browser, a scroll driven replay of the Arup case, and full responsiveness down to 320px.
