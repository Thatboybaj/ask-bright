# Tenuta Palmeri

Winery Concept

Johannes Röhrenbach

Art direction, Web design

A website concept and cross-estate digital system for a Sicilian wine estate with roots in Italy, Spain and Switzerland.

View on Contra

© Tenuta Palmeri

## Overview

Tenuta Palmeri is a Sicilian wine estate within a Swiss-owned group operating wineries in Italy, Spain, and Switzerland. They needed a distinctive, culturally intelligent website direction that moves beyond the typical “luxury winery” aesthetic — while still supporting real operational needs:

~200 wine products

Stripe e-commerce

Events (via Winearound)

Accommodation bookings (via Lodgify/Octorate)

Bilingual content (IT/EN)

Reusable system for future estates in the group

My role was to create a concept exploration that feels contemporary, distinctive, and structurally realistic enough to be included in a formal proposal.

## Objectives

Create a non-generic visual direction that reflects Mediterranean character and avoids winery clichés.

Integrate the existing Palmeri palm symbol into a flexible graphic system, rather than treating it as decorative.

Explore how the system supports: a dense wine shop, hospitality & experiences, estate storytelling.

Design with scalability in mind so the approach can extend to other estates in the group.

Ensure everything works across responsive breakpoints with consistent rhythm and hierarchy.

## Approach & Concept

The concept direction focused on building a tactile Mediterranean atmosphere through:

a grounded, editorial typographic scale

warm, textured color tones

structural use of the palm motif (cropped, layered, or partially used for depth)

photography that feels agricultural and place-driven rather than lifestyle-driven

a layout system that balances storytelling + commerce

The goal: make the estate feel real, rooted, and memorable — without being ornamental or over-designed.

## Key Design Explorations

## 1. Homepage Direction

Developed a strong hero section that establishes tone without relying on literal labels (“wine,” “hospitality,” etc.). The page introduces the estate through mood, rhythm, and visual tension — letting users feel the three pillars (wine, experiences, agriturismo) intuitively.

## 2. Wine Shop (High-Density Grid)

Designed a clean, scalable product grid accommodating 12–16 products per view, ensuring:

clarity at density

consistent hierarchy

easy future scalability for ~200 products

integration with Stripe workflows

## 3. Hospitality & Experiences

Created layouts for rooms & suites, dining, vineyard experiences, and tastings & events. Pages use an editorial collage system paired with structured content blocks to capture both intimacy and clarity.

## 4. Structural Palm Motif

Explored how the existing palm logo could create depth, act as a framing device, and guide rhythm across sections — without becoming ornamental or duplicating the logo.

## 5. Full Mobile Translation

Ensured the entire system translated cleanly across breakpoints, preserving spacing logic, hierarchy, typographic rhythm, and navigation clarity.

## Outcome

A cohesive cross-estate design system

A distinctive and memorable art direction

Working homepage, shop, and hospitality pages

Full mobile design

A flexible visual identity based on the existing palm symbol

A strong proposal-ready presentation for the client

The client was pleased with the clarity, responsiveness, and usability of the system, and the exploration is now being used in a formal proposal to secure the full project.
