# Noah Elliot

Personal Portfolio

'25

Concept

A personal portfolio website communicating design philosophy, services and selected work.

© Noah Elliot

## Project Overview

I designed and developed my personal portfolio website to clearly communicate my design philosophy, services, and work. The goal was to create a minimal yet expressive digital presence that reflects how I approach design as clarity, translation, and emotion — not decoration.

## Challenge

As a personal brand, the site needed to balance simplicity with depth. The main challenge was translating abstract values — identity, emotion, and purpose — into a clear user experience, while maintaining strong visual consistency from design to development. I also wanted full fidelity between Figma and Framer without sacrificing responsiveness or performance.

## Design Approach

I started in Figma by defining a visual identity built on typography, spacing, and balance. The layout focuses on hierarchy and flow: a clear hero statement, followed by services, selected work, and a direct call to action. Copy and visuals were treated as one system, ensuring the messaging reinforced the design concept.

## Key design goals

Clear narrative from landing to contact

Minimal, confident visual language

Strong typography and whitespace for clarity

## Development in Framer

After finalizing the designs, I rebuilt the site in Framer, translating the layouts, components, and spacing with high accuracy. Framer allowed me to maintain design precision while ensuring responsiveness across devices. I implemented reusable components, smooth layouts, and clean structure to keep the site performant and easy to evolve.

## Outcome

The result is a modern, conversion-focused portfolio that communicates both my skill set and design mindset. It functions as a professional showcase, a personal brand statement, and a foundation I can scale with new projects and content over time.

## Key Takeaways

Strong design starts with clear thinking and messaging

Designing and developing the same product ensures consistency

Simplicity, when intentional, creates impact

A modern, conversion-focused portfolio that works as a professional showcase and personal brand statement, ready to scale with new work.
