# Sandline Films

Bilingual cinematic website for a Doha film agency

Sandline Films (self initiated)

Brand, Web design, Development, Motion

A concept film agency in Doha that shoots brand, tourism and documentary work across Qatar. I wrote the brief, designed the brand and built the site in English and Arabic, with films managed in a headless CMS.

© Sandline Films

## Overview

Sandline is a fictional film agency based in Doha. I took it from a written brand and website brief to a deployed, bilingual site, then made a launch reel and a week of social content around it.

## My role

Brand and website brief, positioning and tone of voice.

Brand identity: sun mark, palette and type system.

Web design for every page in both languages.

Astro build in TypeScript, Storyblok CMS setup and Vercel deployment.

Motion system, launch reel and social content.

## Design approach

One dark ground for every section so the letterbox bars never seam. Gold for action, sand and ivory for text, teal and sea for the coast. Grenze Gotisch carries the English headlines, Rakkas the Arabic ones, with Manrope and IBM Plex Sans Arabic for body copy. Arabic is never split into letters, letter-spaced or uppercased, because that breaks the joined script.

## Pages

Home: hero reel, statement, selected films, process and behind-the-scenes.

Films: a screening room where pointing at a title crossfades its footage, with filters.

Seven film pages: player, credits, frames and a preview of the next film.

Services with a map of Qatar drawn from real coastline data, About, Contact and Credits.

Every page in English and Arabic, plus a bilingual 404.

## Motion

A projector-gate preloader with frame weave, flicker and a real loading line.

Page cuts: black bars close, the next page loads behind them, and they open again.

Sticky film cards that slide over each other and part as you scroll.

End-credits footer that shows today’s golden hour in Doha, calculated live.

A Pause motion button, and no preloader, pinning or autoplay under reduced motion.

## Build

Astro and TypeScript, GSAP with ScrollTrigger, and Lenis for smooth scrolling.

Storyblok with English and Arabic content, a visual editor preview and a one-time setup script. Publishing triggers a Vercel rebuild.

Logical CSS properties throughout, so one stylesheet mirrors the whole layout for RTL.

Arabic-Indic digits, hreflang alternates, a sitemap and alt text on every frame.

Background videos only play on screen, with long cache headers for media.

A site that behaves like a film

A brand built on the line where sand meets sea: a setting-sun mark, gold on near-black, and blackletter display type that reads like an old film title card.

An Astro site in English and Arabic (RTL) with seven film pages managed in Storyblok, GSAP and Lenis motion that borrows from the edit suite, and full reduced-motion support. Deployed on Vercel.
