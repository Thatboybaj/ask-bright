# SABLE — Framer template for an independent record label

SABLE is a Framer template Bright designed for independent record labels. It is listed on the Framer Marketplace and Contra.

## What was built
- A complete design system, CMS architecture, sample copy and pricing guidance.
- 11 pages: 8 static pages and 3 CMS templates.
- 4 CMS collections so labels can manage artists, releases and gallery content without touching the layout.
- Navigation: Home, Artists, Releases, Gallery. Footer: Contact and a custom 404.

## Why it matters
The CMS structure lets a small label add a new release or artist in minutes, which is the main job a label site has to do.
